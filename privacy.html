AI Lister eBay Media Privacy Policy

Lone Star Book Rescue operates AI Lister as a private desktop tool. When the seller connects eBay, AI Lister uses the seller’s permission to upload listing photos and retrieve their image URLs. This connection does not request buyer, order, or payment information.

The sign-in return page is hosted by Cloudflare. The page does not store or forward the sign-in code. AI Lister’s local setup exchanges the code directly with eBay and stores the refresh token encrypted in the current Windows user profile while the connection is in use.

You can revoke access in eBay account settings and remove the saved connection on your PC. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact info@conroebookdonations.com.
